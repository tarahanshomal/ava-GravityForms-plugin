# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-05

- Native Gravity Forms SMS feeds with phone-field mapping or up to five fixed recipients, merge tags and conditional logic.
- Persian connection settings, Iranian phone normalization and optional `AVA_GF_API_KEY` override.
- HTTPS Bearer-token requests, idempotency keys, durable duplicate guards and review metadata; no automatic uncertain resend.
- Synthetic behavioral and distribution checks, CI and deterministic installable ZIP releases.
